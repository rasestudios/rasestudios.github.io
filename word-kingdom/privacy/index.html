Privacy Policy

  • Effective date: 14 October 2026
  • Last updated: 14 October 2026
Türkçe metni oku

This privacy policy explains how personal data is processed when you use the mobile game Word Kingdom: Solitaire (in Turkish: Kelime Krallığı: Solitaire; the “App”) on Android or iOS. It also serves as the information notice required by the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”) and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (“KVKK”).

At a glance

  • There is no account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, e-mail address or phone number in the App.
  • We run no servers of our own. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The App is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google processes certain device and usage data to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first and you can change it at any time.
  • The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • The App contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own.

2. Data stored only on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the App saves the following in its private storage on your device:

  • your level progress and the stars you earned;
  • your coin balance;
  • the game in progress, so you can resume an unfinished level;
  • your Daily Challenge history and streak;
  • your settings: language, sound effects, vibration and whether the tutorial has been shown;
  • whether you own the Remove Ads purchase;
  • your ad consent choices (saved by Google’s consent tool, see section 4).

This data is not sent to us; we have no server that could receive it. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data. If you have turned on your device’s own backup (Android backup or iCloud), your operating system may include this data in your backup. That backup is controlled by Google or Apple under your account, not by us.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free because it shows ads: a banner at the bottom of the screen, occasional full-screen ads between levels and, only when you choose to watch one, reward videos (for example for extra moves or a hint). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (for users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland: Google Ireland Limited). The Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the App starts only after the consent step described in section 4.

To deliver ads, measure their performance, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud and abuse, and, only where you allow it, to personalise ads, the SDK may collect:

  • Device identifiers: on Android, the advertising ID and the app set ID; on iOS, the identifier for vendors (IDFV) and, only if you allow tracking (section 5), the advertising identifier (IDFA). Where consent is required (section 4), the advertising ID is used only if you consent; otherwise Google serves non-personalised or “limited” ads.
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country or city level). The App never accesses your precise (GPS) location.
  • Device and app information: for example device model, operating system and version, language, and app version.
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown, tapped or watched (for example whether a reward video was completed).
  • Diagnostics: for example SDK errors and ad loading times.

Google uses this data under its own privacy policy, generally as an independent controller, and, if you consent, shares it with its advertising partners. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you: we only see aggregated reports in AdMob, such as the number of ads shown and the resulting earnings. Ad content in the App is limited to a general-audience rating.

4. Your consent choices (Google UMP)

When the App starts, it uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check whether a privacy message must be shown in your region. No ad is requested before this step is complete.

  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: on first launch you see a consent form. You can consent, refuse, or choose by purpose and by ad partner. If you refuse, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads.
  • U.S. states with consumer privacy laws (for example California): you can opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of your personal information for targeted advertising.

You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Privacy Settings. This option is shown whenever it applies in your region. Your choices are stored on your device. If the App cannot reach Google on its first launch (for example while offline), it shows no ads until the consent step succeeds, and it tries again when you come back to the App.

5. iOS App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, the App may first show a short explanation from Google and then Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt, which asks whether the App may track you across other companies’ apps and websites. The IDFA is available to the ad SDK only if you tap “Allow”. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, ads are still shown but are not personalised using the IDFA.

You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

6. In-app purchases

The optional one-time Remove Ads purchase is sold through Google Play Billing on Android and the Apple App Store on iOS. The payment is processed entirely by Google or Apple: we never see your card number, billing address or other payment details.

  • The App only receives the purchase status from the store (for example that Remove Ads is owned, pending or refunded) and saves ownership on your device. Each time the App starts, it quietly asks the store which purchases you own, so the purchase comes back after a reinstall and is removed again after a refund.
  • Google and Apple provide us with transaction reports (for example an order number, product, price, date and country or region). We use them only for accounting, tax and refund handling.

Store privacy policies: Google · Apple.

7. Contacting support

If you e-mail us (for example through Settings → Contact Support, which pre-fills the subject with the App name and version), we process your e-mail address, your message and anything you choose to include, only to answer your request. Our e-mail provider stores these messages on our behalf.

8. What we do not collect

  • No account, name, e-mail address or phone number is requested in the App.
  • The App does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or location.
  • The App contains no analytics, crash-reporting or social media SDKs.
  • We do not sell your personal data for money, and we do not build profiles about you ourselves.

10. Recipients and international transfers

  • Google (AdMob, the UMP consent tool and Google Play Billing) and, if you consent, Google’s advertising partners;
  • Apple (App Store and its purchase system) on iOS;
  • our e-mail provider, for support messages;
  • public authorities, only where the law requires it.

These companies may process data on servers outside Türkiye and the European Economic Area, for example in the United States. Such transfers take place in line with Chapter V of the GDPR and Article 9 of the KVKK, for example on the basis of adequacy decisions (such as the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework for certified companies), standard contractual clauses or standard contracts, or, for occasional transfers where no other safeguard applies, your explicit consent.

We do not sell your personal data for money. Under some U.S. state laws, letting ad partners use data for targeted advertising can count as a “sale” or “sharing”; you can opt out as described in section 4.

11. How long data is kept

  • Game data and consent choices on your device: until you uninstall the App or clear its data (consent choices also change when you update them).
  • Advertising data: kept by Google according to its retention policy.
  • Purchase records: kept by Google or Apple under their policies; the transaction reports we receive are kept as long as accounting and tax laws require.
  • Support e-mails: kept while we handle your request and deleted no later than 12 months after it is resolved, unless we need them longer to establish or defend legal claims.

12. Your rights and how to use them

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict its processing, to data portability, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before.

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed; to request information about it; to learn the purpose of the processing and whether the data is used accordingly; to know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred; to request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data; to request its deletion or destruction under the conditions of Article 7; to request that these corrections and deletions be notified to those third parties; to object to a result against you that arises solely from automated analysis; and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

If you live in a U.S. state with a consumer privacy law, you may have the right to know, access, correct and delete personal information, and to opt out of its “sale” or “sharing” for targeted advertising. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

How to make a request

E-mail support@rasestudios.com with the subject “Privacy request”. We answer free of charge and within 30 days at the latest. Because the App has no accounts and we keep no data about you on any server, we usually cannot link advertising data to you. The fastest controls are:

  • in the App: Settings → Privacy Settings (consent choices);
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads on newer versions, to reset or delete your advertising ID (menu names vary by device);
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking;
  • if you have a Google account: My Ad Center;
  • uninstalling the App deletes all data it stored on your device.

Complaints

You can complain to a data protection authority: in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK), within 30 days of receiving our reply and in any case within 60 days of your request; in the EEA, the authority of your country; in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office. We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

13. Children

The App is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. It is not part of Google Play’s “Designed for Families” programme or Apple’s Kids category, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads are limited to a general-audience content rating. If you are below the age of digital consent in your country (between 13 and 16 in the EEA), please ask a parent or guardian before agreeing to personalised ads. If you believe a child’s data has been processed, contact us and we will help.

14. Security

Data on your device is kept in the App’s private storage, which the operating system protects from other apps. Communication with the advertising and store services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Because we run no servers and keep no database of players, there is no central copy of your game data that could be exposed. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we limit data to what the App needs.

15. This website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics, trackers, external scripts or external fonts. The hosting provider, GitHub, Inc., may log technical data such as your IP address to operate and secure its service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Links to Google, Apple and other sites lead to services with their own privacy policies.

16. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the App or the law changes, for example if we add a new service provider. The current version is always published on this page with its “Last updated” date. For significant changes we will also inform you in the App’s release notes or on its store page.
